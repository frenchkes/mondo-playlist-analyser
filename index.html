01 / Import your playlist

Spotify link importing is not enabled in this first version. Spotify's current developer restrictions prevent a new independent app from reliably reading arbitrary playlist tracks or their audio-analysis data. Use a CSV export or paste your track list below; no Spotify password is requested.

CSV columns supported: title, artist, bpm, key (Camelot such as 8A), energy. You can also edit every value after import.

02 / Free BPM & Camelot analysis

MONDO uses TuneBad's free playlist analyser for BPM, musical key and Camelot. No paid API key or subscription.

In TuneBad, paste your public Spotify playlist link, tap Analyze playlist, then choose Export CSV. Return here and import that CSV above. MONDO will read TuneBad's BPM and Camelot columns automatically.

Important: TuneBad currently provides BPM and key/Camelot, but not a measured energy score. MONDO leaves Energy at 5 until you rate it; it won't pretend that tempo is the same as energy. The CSV hand-off is one manual export/import step because TuneBad does not publish an integration API.

06 / Harmonic mixing reminder
